Privacy Policy — Roz ki Dua

Last updated: August 2026

Roz ki Dua ("the App") is a free Islamic app that shows daily duas, Surahs, Tasbih (dhikr counter), Rohani Ilaj (categorized duas), and prayer (Namaz) timings. This page explains what information the App accesses and how it is used.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Roz ki Dua does not have a server or a database of its own. We do not collect, store, or have access to your name, email, phone number, or any personal identity information. There is no user account or sign-up required to use the App.

2. Location Access

If you choose to use the "Namaz ke Waqt" (prayer times) feature, the App asks for your device's location. This location is used only to calculate accurate prayer timings for your area by sending your coordinates to a third-party prayer-times service (Aladhan API). Your location is stored only on your own device (in local browser storage) so the App doesn't need to ask again every day, and is never sent to us or stored on any server we control.

3. Notifications

If you turn on "Roz Reminder", the App asks for notification permission to send you a daily reminder. This works entirely on your device — no reminder data is sent anywhere.

4. Data Stored on Your Device

The App saves the following information only on your own device (using browser local storage), so that your preferences are remembered between visits:

  • Duas and Ayahs you have saved or liked
  • Your language preference (Hindi + English, or English only)
  • Your Tasbih (dhikr) counts
  • Your Roz Reminder time setting
  • Your last-used location, if you enabled Namaz ke Waqt

None of this information leaves your device or is shared with us. If you uninstall the App or clear your browser data, this information is deleted.

5. Third-Party Services Used

To show Quran text, translations, audio recitation, and prayer timings, the App fetches data from these external, publicly available services when you use those features:

  • alquran.cloud — Quran text, translation, and transliteration
  • Aladhan API — Prayer (Namaz) timings based on your location

These services may have their own privacy practices. We do not control what these third parties do with requests sent to them.

6. Advertising

The App does not currently show any advertisements. If this changes in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. Since no personal data is collected at all, the App is safe for users of all ages.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the App adds new features. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out through the app's GitHub page: github.com/Shaheen-duas/Roz-ki-dua
